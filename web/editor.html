Program editor

You write the rules, not the moves. Rules are checked top to bottom every tick, and the first one that matches decides what the robot does.
Actions come in two kinds. A primary action — moving, navigating, picking up, attacking — is the robot's move for that tick and ends the tick: no further rule is checked. The seven side effects (five memory writes and two broadcasts) cost no time: they run and checking continues down the list, so a rule can remember something and a lower rule still gets to act on the same tick. What was remembered can be read from the next tick onwards.

    Design a robot

    Exactly one locomotion unit and one armored body; at most one radar and two weapons. A manipulator is what lets a robot pick anything up. Every part you add is mass, and mass is speed.
