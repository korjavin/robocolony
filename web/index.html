robocolony

Autonomous robot colony simulation — pre-alpha.

You are an engineer of behavior, not a unit commander. You design robot blueprints and ordered priority-rule programs, then your colony competes on a procedurally generated arena against other colonies — including while you are offline.

  • Robots are never steered directly. They execute rules.
  • Perception is limited: forward vision, optional radars, simple signals.
  • The simulation runs server-side and is watchable live.

Nothing is playable yet. This page is the deployment smoke test.
